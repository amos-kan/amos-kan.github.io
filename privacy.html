Shorts uploader privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026.

Shorts uploader is a personal tool that uploads its owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It runs only on the owner's computer.

YouTube API Services

Shorts uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to your channel's name, statistics and list of uploads (youtube.readonly), to confirm which channel videos go to and to check an upload's status.

What it stores, and where

The sign-in token Google issues is saved in a file on the owner's computer, and a log of each upload (file name, video ID, title, privacy setting and time) is saved next to the uploaded video. Nothing is stored on any server run by Shorts uploader, because there is none.

Sharing

Shorts uploader sends data only to Google, through the YouTube Data API, to upload videos and read the channel details above. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone else, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

Removing access and data

You can revoke Shorts uploader's access at any time on your Google Account's Third-party connections page (also reachable from Google security settings). Deleting the token file on the computer removes the stored sign-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support email shown on the Shorts uploader sign-in screen.
